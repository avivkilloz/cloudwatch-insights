Before this app can assume roles in any target AWS account:
  1. Make sure the ServiceAccount ({{ include "cloudwatch-insights.serviceAccountName" . }})
     is annotated with a valid IRSA role ARN
     (Values.serviceAccount.annotations."eks.amazonaws.com/role-arn").
  2. That IAM role must be allowed to sts:AssumeRole into the role you plan
     to configure per account (e.g. CloudWatchInsightsReadRole).
  3. Each target account must have that role created, trusting this hub
     role's ARN.
See the top-level README's "Kubernetes / IRSA prerequisites" section for
the full walkthrough and example policies.

Once it's reachable, open the "Accounts & Settings" tab in the app to set
the role name and add your accounts.
